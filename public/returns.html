Returns & refunds

Last updated November 3, 2026

30-day returns

If you're not happy with your Phoenix PC, you can return it within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, contact us with your order reference and we'll send you a prepaid return label and packing instructions.

Condition

Returned PCs should come back in their original packaging with all accessories, without physical damage, and with no parts removed or swapped. We inspect every return when it arrives.

Refunds

  • Prebuilt rigs: full refund of the purchase price.
  • Custom builds: refund of the purchase price minus a 10% restocking fee, which covers the parts we ordered for your configuration.
  • Defective or damaged on arrival: full refund or replacement, always with no restocking fee.

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving and inspecting the return. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any order for a full refund before it ships. Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. Once a PC has shipped, the normal return process applies.

Exceptions

We can't accept returns for damage caused by misuse, accidents, liquid, or unauthorised modifications that damage parts. Software you installed and personal data are your responsibility to remove before sending the PC back.

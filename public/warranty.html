Policies

3-year warranty

Last updated November 3, 2026

What's covered

Every Phoenix PC comes with a 3-year limited warranty on parts and labor, starting on the delivery date. If a component fails under normal use, we'll diagnose it and repair or replace it at no charge, including shipping both ways in the first year.

Lifetime support

You get free technical help for the life of your PC, whether it's a driver issue, a game that won't run or advice on upgrades. Contact us anytime.

Upgrades don't void your warranty

You're free to open your case, clean it and add or upgrade parts. Your warranty stays in place for all original parts. We only exclude damage caused while upgrading, and the parts you add yourself.

What's not covered

  • Accidental damage, drops, liquid spills or power surges
  • Damage from overclocking beyond the settings we shipped
  • Cosmetic wear such as scratches on panels
  • Software problems, viruses or data loss. Please back up your files.
  • Parts you install yourself, which are covered by their own manufacturer warranty

How to make a claim

Message us with your order reference and a description of the problem. Most issues can be solved remotely. If your PC needs to come back to us, we'll send a prepaid label and aim to return it within 7 business days of it arriving.

Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Phoenix PCs ("we", "us") collects when you use this website or buy from us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), build configuration and any build notes you enter at checkout.
  • Shipping and billing details: your shipping and billing addresses and phone number, collected on Stripe's secure checkout page.
  • Payment information: card details are entered directly on Stripe's page and processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form.
  • Your cart: stored only in your own browser (local storage) until you check out or clear it.

How we use it

We use your information to build, ship and support your order, to send order and shipping updates, to answer your questions, to handle returns and warranty claims, and to prevent fraud. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, to process payments (Stripe privacy policy)
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your PC
  • Netlify, which hosts this website and stores order records and contact messages
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts on this site and may receive your IP address
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

Cookies & tracking

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe may set cookies on its checkout page for fraud prevention.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for your warranty and for tax and accounting obligations, usually up to 7 years. Contact messages are deleted after 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have more rights under local law. To make a request, contact us.

Children

Purchases must be made by adults aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.

Policies

Shipping policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Build & dispatch times

Every Phoenix PC is assembled to order. Most builds ship within 7 business days of payment. That includes assembly, hand cable management, software setup and a 48-hour stress test. Larger custom-loop builds can take up to 10 business days. If a part is delayed, we'll email you with an updated date and give you the option to swap the part or cancel for a full refund.

Shipping cost & carriers

Shipping is free on every order within the 50 US states. We ship by insured 2-day ground or air with a major carrier, and an adult signature is required on delivery. You'll get an email with your tracking number as soon as your PC leaves our bench.

Packaging

Your PC ships in a double-walled box with custom foam inserts. Expanding foam inside the case and a GPU support bracket protect the parts in transit. Remove the internal foam before powering on; the setup card in the box shows how.

Where we ship

We currently ship to residential and business addresses in the 50 US states. We can't ship to PO boxes, APO/FPO addresses or outside the US.

Damaged or lost shipments

Every shipment is fully insured. If your package arrives damaged, take photos of the box and its contents and contact us within 7 days of delivery. We'll arrange a repair or replacement at no cost. If tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, let us know and we'll open a claim with the carrier.

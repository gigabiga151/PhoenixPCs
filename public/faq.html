Support

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get my PC?

Most builds ship within 7 business days of payment. That covers assembly, cable management and our 48-hour stress test. Delivery then takes 2 business days with our free insured shipping. If a part is backordered, we'll email you before anything changes.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. You enter your card on Stripe's encrypted page and Phoenix PCs never sees or stores your full card number.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover credit and debit cards, plus Google Pay and Apple Pay on supported devices.

Can I change my build after ordering?

Usually, yes, as long as we haven't started assembly. Contact us with your order reference and we'll confirm the change and any price difference.

Do your PCs come with Windows?

Yes. Every Phoenix ships with Windows 11 Home installed, activated and updated, plus the latest drivers. There's no bloatware or trial software.

What does the 48-hour stress test involve?

Each PC runs CPU, GPU and memory stress loads and a thermal soak for two full days. We check temperatures, clocks and stability, then do a final gaming benchmark before it ships.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We currently ship to addresses in the 50 US states.

What if something arrives damaged?

Take photos of the box and the PC, then contact us within 7 days of delivery. Every shipment is fully insured, so we'll repair or replace it at no cost to you.

Can I upgrade my PC later?

Absolutely. We use standard parts and leave room for upgrades. Opening your case or adding parts doesn't void your warranty. Only damage caused during the upgrade isn't covered.

How do I track my order?

Use the 8-character order reference from your confirmation screen and email receipt on the order tracker.
